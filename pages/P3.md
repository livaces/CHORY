  (a) $$f\space{:}\space\R\to\R^{2}\space{,}\space(x,y)\mapsto(x+y,x-y)$$
	- Sejam $$\overrightarrow{u}=(x_{1},y_{1}){,}\space\overrightarrow{v}=(x_{2},y_{2})\in\R^{2}$$ e $$\lambda\in\R$$
	  
	  Temos, assim:
	  1.
	  \begin{split}
	  f(\overrightarrow{u}+\overrightarrow{v})
	  &= f(x_{1}+x_{2},\, y_{1}+y_{2}) \\
	  &= (x_1+x_2+y_1+y_2,x_1+x_2-y_1-y_2) \\
	  &= (x_{1}+y_{1},\, x_{1}-y_{1}) + (x_{2}+y_{2},\, x_{2}-y_{2}) \\
	  &= f(x_1,y_1)+f(x_2,y_2) \\
	  &= f(\overrightarrow{u})+f(\overrightarrow{v})
	  \end{split}
	  2.
	  \begin{split}
	  f(\lambda\overrightarrow{u})
	  &= f(\lambda x_{1},\lambda y_{1}) \\
	  &= (\lambda x_1+\lambda y_1,\lambda x_1-\lambda y_1) \\
	  &= \lambda(x_{1}+y_{1},x_{1}-y_{1}) \\
	  &= \lambda f(\overrightarrow{u})
	  \end{split}
